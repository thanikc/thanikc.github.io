# Food preferences

Thanik Cheowtirakul enjoys a wide range of food and is usually happy to try
something new. His clear favourites are Thai, Japanese, and Cantonese cooking.
The dishes Thanik could eat almost every day are pad see ew, khao soi, and dim
sum, and an occasional plate of A5 wagyu such as Matsusaka beef genuinely makes
his day.

Thanik is especially fond of street food: sticky rice with moo ping (grilled
pork skewers) and takoyaki are favourites of his, and fresh seafood is always
welcome. Outside Asian cuisine, Thanik also enjoys a good spread of tapas and a
properly made paella. The list goes on, but that covers the highlights.
