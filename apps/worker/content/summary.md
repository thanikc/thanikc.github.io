# Summary

Thanik Cheowtirakul is a full-stack software engineer with more than 25 years of
professional experience, working in IT since 1999 and as a developer in the
banking and finance sector since 2010. Since 2016 he has been a Lead Programmer at
attempto GmbH & Co. KG, and he has spent much of the last decade building online
banking applications for the German cooperative-banking sector at clients such as
Atruvia AG and Fiducia & GAD IT AG.

His core stack is enterprise Angular on the front end and Spring Boot
microservices on the back end, deployed on OpenShift and Kubernetes. He
specialises in micro-frontend architectures built with Angular and web
components, front-end performance work (server-side rendering, zoneless change
detection, and a full move to Signals), and CI/CD pipelines. Thanik works
test-first with tools such as Playwright and Cypress, collaborates in Scrum and
Kanban teams, and has helped introduce AI-assisted development as a team
standard. He holds a BSc in Computer Science from Technische Hochschule
Mittelhessen.
