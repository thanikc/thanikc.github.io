# Interests and hobbies

Outside of work, Thanik Cheowtirakul stays active and keeps learning.

## Swimming

Thanik swims regularly, mainly for endurance rather than speed, and is always
working on improving his technique and going further.

## Bouldering

After a recurring spinal injury, Thanik stopped training Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and
recently took up bouldering instead. It lets him keep doing something physical
and maintain core strength while also solving problems on the wall, which is a
big part of why he enjoys it.

## Chess

Thanik plays chess occasionally.

## Learning Mandarin

Thanik has recently started learning Mandarin Chinese, adding to the German,
English, and Thai he already speaks.
