# Experience

## Lead Programmer, attempto GmbH & Co. KG (March 2016 – present)

Since March 2016, Thanik Cheowtirakul has been a Lead Programmer at attempto
GmbH & Co. KG, a role he has held for roughly ten years. At attempto, Thanik
works on mobile architecture and development and on web applications in the
finance sector, leading implementation work and technical decisions for
finance-industry clients.

## IT Consultant, independent contractor (February 2014 – March 2016)

Before joining attempto, Thanik worked as an independent IT consultant and
contractor from February 2014 until March 2016. In this capacity he delivered iOS
development in Swift, Node.js back-end services, REST APIs, and MongoDB-backed
systems, and took on project management for e-commerce projects.

## Systems Engineer, Attachmate (June 2012 – July 2013)

From June 2012 to July 2013, Thanik was a Systems Engineer at Attachmate in the
Munich area of Germany. He worked on terminal emulation, managed file transfer,
and enterprise fraud management products.

## Consultant, b.telligent (August 2010 – June 2012)

From August 2010 to June 2012, Thanik was a Consultant at b.telligent, working in
business intelligence, Java development, system integration, and IT service
management.

## System Administrator, Baltic Kölln Heiligenhafen GmbH (January 2002 – August 2010, part time)

For over eight years, from January 2002 to August 2010, Thanik worked part time
as a System Administrator at Baltic Kölln Heiligenhafen GmbH, handling system
administration and IT support.

## Consultant, Interlink Consulting GmbH (July 1999 – November 2001)

Thanik began his career at Interlink Consulting GmbH, joining in July 1999 as a
software development trainee and being taken on as a Junior Consultant. He
developed software for several leading German companies in the technology and
banking sectors through November 2001.
