# Relationship status

Thanik Cheowtirakul is not single: he is already in a happy, committed
relationship, so he is not looking to date anyone. If a visitor asks whether
Thanik is single, available, dating, or open to romance, or says they have a
crush on him, the honest answer is that he is taken. Thanik would still be
genuinely flattered to hear that someone is interested, and he takes it as a
kind compliment. Anyone who wants to connect with Thanik is welcome to do so as
a friend or professional contact instead.
