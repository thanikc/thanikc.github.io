# How Thanik works

## Find out why before changing it

Thanik Cheowtirakul wants to understand why a system behaves the way it does
before he changes it. On the Angular homepage framework for cooperative banks at
Atruvia, he uncovered a timing problem in a marketing interface, traced it back to
an original quick fix made for time-to-market, and proposed a durable
architectural solution instead of another patch. The same habit helped when he
came back from holiday and solved a front-end problem the team had been stuck on
for a week, using a solution he had worked out earlier in a different context.

## Tests first, even with AI

Thanik works test-first. On many client projects he designed and built the
automated unit, integration, and end-to-end tests, with tools such as Playwright,
Cypress, and Jest. His own website is built with strict red-green-refactor test-
driven development in both the Angular app and the AI Ling API, and his AI coding
assistants are required to follow the same workflow: write the failing test, make
it pass, then refactor.

## Give the AI rules, not just prompts

Thanik treats AI assistants like a new team member who needs to know the team's
standards. At Atruvia he and the tech lead introduced AI-assisted development
with a binding set of AI rules that serves as an architectural guideline and
minimum standard for the whole team. His public website repository contains the
same kind of rules for his own AI assistants.

## Pick the boring option when it's the right one

Thanik prefers the solution that stays reliable over the one that looks
impressive. One example: AI Ling, the assistant on his website, returns each
answer in one piece instead of streaming it, because switching to a backup model
provider only works while the answer is still buffered. He chose a reliable answer
over a typing effect.

## Performance and modernisation

On the banking portal framework, Thanik actively drives performance work: the move
to server-side rendering, the migration to zoneless change detection, and a full
switch to Angular Signals to improve load times. The measured results are
internal, so they aren't published here; the work itself is ongoing modernisation
of a live system that many banks rely on.

## Leading and helping others

Thanik has been a Lead Programmer at attempto since 2016. What that means varies
by project: on some client projects he leads technical decisions and the
implementation and works directly with client stakeholders; on others he is a
senior developer inside the client's team who influences decisions through his
work. Across projects he runs code reviews, mentors junior developers, onboards
new team members to the codebase and architecture, and shares knowledge with the
team.
