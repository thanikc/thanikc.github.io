# Projects

A reverse-chronological record of the client and consulting projects Thanik
Cheowtirakul has delivered as a developer, mostly in the banking and finance
sector.

## Homepage framework for banking portals — Atruvia AG (January 2025 – present)

As a developer at Atruvia AG, Thanik works on the central homepage framework used
by banks in the German cooperative-banking (Genossenschaftsbanken) sector. The
Angular-based portal lets each bank configure and maintain its own content from
Adobe Experience Manager (the CMS), offers configurable navigation, and gives
every bank a shared base design that they can fully customise in layout and
appearance. External web applications can be embedded into the portal as web
components.

Thanik is a core member of the five-person team for the Angular front-end
development. He contributes to architectural decisions — for example, he
uncovered a timing problem in a marketing interface (rooted in an original
"quick fix" made for time-to-market) and proposed a durable architectural
solution. He actively drives performance work on the application: the move to
server-side rendering (SSR), migration to zoneless change detection, and a full
switch to Signals to improve load times. He runs regular code reviews with the
team. Together with the tech lead he introduced AI-assisted development,
producing a binding set of AI rules that serves as an architectural guideline and
minimum standard for the whole team. He has a deep understanding of the codebase
beyond his own area: returning from holiday, he solved a front-end problem the
team had been stuck on for a week, drawing on a solution he had worked out
earlier in a different context.

Skills: Angular, Handlebars, Jest, AI-assisted software development, NestJS, Nx,
Playwright, Scrum.
