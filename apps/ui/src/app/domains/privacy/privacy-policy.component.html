2. Overview of Data Processing

This site does not ask you to create an account or submit a form. Personal data is processed in three situations: standard technical access logs generated by our hosting provider, the optional analytics service described below, which only activates after you consent via the cookie banner, and, if you choose to use it, the built-in AI assistant, which sends what you type to our backend and to a third-party AI provider as described in "AI Assistant (Chat)" below.

The retirement calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser: the numbers you enter are never sent to us or stored anywhere, and the only outgoing request it makes is to the public World Bank API to fetch economic indicators, which does not receive any of your inputs.

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). Like most web hosts, GitHub automatically processes technical information for every request in server access logs, which typically includes your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request. This processing is necessary to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse, and is based on our legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not have access to these raw logs ourselves; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. AI Assistant (Chat)

This site includes an AI chat assistant that answers questions about Thanik and his work. If you type a message, that message and the recent conversation history from the same session are sent from your browser to our own backend, a serverless function running on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.). The backend does not store the text of your messages or log their content. If a message opens a conversation, we keep the generated answer, along with a hash and a numeric fingerprint (an embedding) of the question, in a Cloudflare D1 database for 30 days, so that the same or a very similar question can be answered without asking an AI provider again. That answer may therefore be shown to other visitors, and it could repeat something you wrote, so please don't include personal details in your messages.

To generate a reply, our backend forwards your message and conversation history to one third-party AI provider per request — whichever of the following is available at the time: Groq, Inc., Google LLC (Google AI), or OpenRouter, Inc. Each provider processes your message to generate the response and may retain it briefly under its own policies for safety and service operation; see Groq's Privacy Policy, Google's Privacy Policy, and OpenRouter's Privacy Policy.

The chat widget itself does not use cookies or local storage: your conversation lives only in your browser's memory for the current page view and is lost on refresh. Because this processing only happens when you actively choose to type a message, it is based on our legitimate interest in providing the feature you are engaging with (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); if you would rather your messages not be processed this way, simply don't use the chat assistant. This processing is not gated by the cookie banner, which controls only Google Analytics.

Because Groq and OpenRouter are based in the United States, using the assistant involves an international transfer of your message data outside the European Economic Area; see "International Data Transfers" below.

For every chat request we also record technical metrics in Cloudflare Analytics Engine: a random request ID, the language, which provider answered, whether the cache was used, the outcome, the response time and the length of the answer. These contain neither your message nor your IP address. To limit abuse, your IP address is also used briefly to count your requests (at most 10 per minute), and Cloudflare may keep technical request logs under its own policies.

The microphone button in the chat uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which only runs after you press the button and allow microphone access. Your browser handles the audio, and some browsers send it to their maker's servers to turn it into text (Chrome, for example, sends it to Google) under that company's own policies. We never receive the audio: only the resulting text reaches us, and only if you send it as a message. This feature is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. Cookies, Local Storage & Your Consent Choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept or reject non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key cookie-consent) so we don't ask again on future visits. This storage is strictly necessary to remember your preference and does not itself require consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / ePrivacy exemption for strictly necessary storage).

Your chosen language is remembered the same way, in your browser's local storage (key preferred-language), purely so the site opens in that language on future visits. Like your cookie choice, this storage is strictly necessary and does not itself require consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / ePrivacy exemption for strictly necessary storage).

Your choice controls Google Analytics: its script is not loaded, and nothing is sent to Google, until you accept. If you reject or later withdraw consent, it is switched off immediately and is not loaded on later visits. The legal basis for this service is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

You can withdraw or change your consent choice at any time, just as easily as giving it, by clicking the button below. This reopens the cookie banner and, until you choose again, denies analytics storage.

6. Google Analytics (GA4)

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site (page views and interactions such as clicks on featured links). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. Data is retained according to the retention setting configured in our Google Analytics property, and Google may combine it with data from other sources per its own privacy policy. Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and stops immediately if you reject or later withdraw consent.

(both US-based) may also process your message data, and Cloudflare, Inc. (also US-based) runs the backend that relays it and holds the answer cache. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, these providers rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. GitHub similarly relies on comparable safeguards for any transfers related to hosting this site.

8. How Long We Keep Data

Your cookie consent choice stays in your browser's local storage until you clear it or click "Manage Cookie Preferences" above. The text of your chat messages is not stored by us: it exists only in your browser's memory during your session and in the backend's memory for the duration of a single request. Cached answers and question fingerprints expire after 30 days and are deleted the next time the cache is written to, and the per-request metrics are kept for as long as Cloudflare Analytics Engine retains them. We do not control retention periods for hosting logs or third-party services directly; see the linked provider policies above for their specific retention practices.
