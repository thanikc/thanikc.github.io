How It's Calculated

📐 Dynamic Formulas

  • Total Goal: (Target Monthly Income × 12) ÷ (Withdrawal Rate ÷ 100)
  • Compounded Net Worth: Current Net Worth × (1 + Return Rate ÷ 100)^Years
  • Remaining Gap: Max(0, Total Goal - Compounded Net Worth)
  • Monthly Savings: Gap × [ Monthly Rate ÷ ((1 + Monthly Rate)^Months - 1) ]

💡 Active Parameters

  • check_circle {{ estimatedAnnualReturn() }}% Return Rate: Compounding rate applied annually to current assets and monthly to savings.
  • check_circle {{ safeWithdrawalRate() }}% Safe Withdrawal Rate: Dictates the target portfolio balance needed to generate income sustainably.
