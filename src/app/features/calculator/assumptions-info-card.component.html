info How It Is Calculated & Underlying Assumptions

1. Target Nest Egg: Based on the standard {{ safeWithdrawalRate() }}% Safe Withdrawal Rate (Trinity Study rule), your required nest egg is calculated as: Target Nest Egg = (Desired Monthly Income × 12) / {{ safeWithdrawalRate() / 100 }}

2. Current Net Worth Growth: Your existing net worth is assumed to compound annually at an estimated rate of {{ estimatedAnnualReturn() }}% over the remaining years until retirement: Future Value of Net Worth = Current Net Worth × (1 + Return Rate)^Years

3. Required Monthly Contribution: The remaining gap between your target nest egg and the future value of your current net worth is funded via monthly contributions compounding over time at {{ estimatedAnnualReturn() }}% per year.
