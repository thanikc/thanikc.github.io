2. Overview of Data Processing

This site does not run its own backend or database, and it does not ask you to create an account or submit a form. Personal data is processed only in two situations: standard technical access logs generated by our hosting provider, and the optional third-party services (fonts, analytics, and advertising) described below, most of which only activate after you consent via the cookie banner.

The retirement calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser: the numbers you enter are never sent to us or stored anywhere, and the only outgoing request it makes is to the public World Bank API to fetch economic indicators, which does not receive any of your inputs.

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). Like most web hosts, GitHub automatically processes technical information for every request in server access logs, which typically includes your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request. This processing is necessary to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse, and is based on our legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not have access to these raw logs ourselves; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Your Consent Choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept or reject non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key cookie-consent) so we don't ask again on future visits. This storage is strictly necessary to remember your preference and does not itself require consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / ePrivacy exemption for strictly necessary storage).

Your choice controls Google Analytics and Google AdSense via Google Consent Mode: both are blocked from storing or reading any analytics or advertising cookies until you accept, and they stop again if you withdraw consent. The legal basis for these two services is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

You can withdraw or change your consent choice at any time, just as easily as giving it, by clicking the button below. This reopens the cookie banner and, until you choose again, denies analytics and advertising storage.

5. Google Fonts

This site loads the Roboto typeface and Material Icons directly from Google's font servers (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Unlike analytics and advertising, these requests are not gated by the cookie banner: they happen on every page load so the page can render with the correct fonts, and in doing so your browser sends your IP address to Google. This processing rests on our legitimate interest in consistent, performant typography (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google acts as an independent controller for this data; see Google's Privacy Policy. You can object to this processing at any time (see "Your Rights" below) or use browser extensions that block third-party font requests.

6. Google Analytics (GA4)

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site (page views and interactions such as clicks on featured links). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. Data is retained according to the retention setting configured in our Google Analytics property, and Google may combine it with data from other sources per its own privacy policy. Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and stops immediately if you reject or later withdraw consent.

7. Google AdSense (Advertising)

If you accept cookies, this site displays advertisements served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance, and — where personalization is enabled — to show ads based on your interests. This processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via the cookie banner above, through Google Ad Settings, or via www.aboutads.info.

Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, Google relies on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. 10. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • request rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • request erasure of your data (Art. 17);
  • request restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, such as Google Fonts (Art. 21); and
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at {{ contactEmail }}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR).
